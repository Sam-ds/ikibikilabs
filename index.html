Why we're building this

The best wellness app is the one you don't have to fight.

Most screen-time apps count your minutes and hand out guilt. We wanted the opposite: something warm enough that protecting your time feels like caring for someone.

A happy buddy is the whole point.
01

A face, not a number

Your buddy's mood is the dashboard. Under your limit, they're happy. Ignore them and they fade — no charts required.

02

Rewards, not restrictions

Focus sessions, calm days, and phone-free hours earn biscuits. Spend them on your pet — not on more screen time.

03

Private by default

Everything stays on your phone. No account, no cloud sync, no ads. Your screen time is nobody else's business.

04

Small enough to keep

No 40-screen onboarding, no subscription maze. One pet, one budget, one calm corner of your phone.
