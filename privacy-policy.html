Privacy Policy

Helpful Pets is built to keep your screen-time data on your phone. This page explains what the app reads, what it stores, and what you can control.

Effective 9 November 2026 Policy version 2026-11-09.1
The short version

On your phone, not on our servers.

Helpful Pets has no account, no cloud sync, no analytics and no ads. Your screen-time data, reminders, habits and optional voice clips stay on your device.

  • Local by default. Your buddy, goals and history are stored in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Permissions are optional. Usage access powers screen-time moods. Microphone and notifications are used only when you choose them.
  • No selling or sharing. We do not collect or sell your data, and there are no advertising SDKs.
01

Quick answers

Does Helpful Pets upload my screen time?

No. Daily totals, pickups and pet progress stay in local app storage.

Does it read my messages or photos?

No. Usage access only sees app foreground time and screen interactions, not screen contents.

Can I use it without permissions?

Yes. Focus sessions, habits and reminders still work. Screen-time moods need usage access.

02

What Helpful Pets reads

Helpful Pets only uses the permissions below to make its features work. You can decline optional permissions and still use much of the app.

Usage access

Optional but needed for screen-time features. Android grants it through Settings > Special app access > Usage access. Helpful Pets reads which app was in the foreground, for how long, and when the screen was interacted with (a pickup). This powers the daily budget, the pet's mood and dedicated-hours checks.

Helpful Pets does not use usage access to read what is on your screen, your messages, photos, files, clipboard or keystrokes. It is not screen recording.

Microphone

Optional. It is only requested when you tap Record in Settings > Pet voice. Helpful Pets records at most five seconds, stores the compressed clip in the app's private storage, and never uploads it. You can deny the microphone and still use Helpful Pets without recorded voice.

Notifications and alarms

Optional. They are used only for the water, tablet and custom reminders you create. Android 13 and later ask for notification permission when you save your first reminder. Android 12 and later may ask you to allow exact alarms. Without that grant, reminders can arrive a few minutes late.

Internet

Internet access is declared only because the 3D pets use a local viewer inside the app. Helpful Pets does not send your data to a server and makes no external network requests.

03

What Helpful Pets stores

  • Your buddy, its name, biscuits, bond level, hunger and wellness.
  • Daily usage totals, focus sessions, pickups and dedicated-hour results.
  • Your limits, focus goal, quiet-hour schedules and habit history.
  • Reminders you create, including titles, medicine names, times and sticker choices.
  • Optional pet voice recordings and their short durations.
  • A consent record with the policy version and the date you accepted it.
Where it lives. All of this is stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. There is no account, no cloud sync, no analytics and no advertising SDK. Android cloud backup and transfer between devices are disabled for Helpful Pets, so this data is not copied to Google backup.
04

What Helpful Pets never collects

  • Message contents, notifications, photos, videos, files or clipboard data.
  • Your contact list, call history, precise location or camera.
  • Microphone audio unless you deliberately record a pet voice clip.
  • Keystrokes, passwords or screenshots of other apps.

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

05

Your controls

  • Revoke Usage Access in Android Settings at any time. Tracking stops, and the pet keeps its current mood but cannot see new screen time until access is granted again.
  • Revoke notification or exact alarm access in Android Settings. Reminders stay saved but may not arrive on time.
  • Delete individual voice clips in Settings > Pet voice, or turn pet voice off entirely.
  • Reset all Helpful Pets data from Settings > Data > Reset everything. This also deletes your reminders, habits and voice recordings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its local data from your device.
  • You can decline Usage Access and still use focus sessions, habits, treats and reminders. The pet will not react to real screen time until you grant it.
Deleting your data. Because everything is on your device, we cannot delete it from a server. Reset the app or uninstall it to remove the data. If you need help, contact us.
06

Notifications and background work

Helpful Pets does not run an always-on background service. Reminders are scheduled with Android's AlarmManager and re-armed after a reboot or app update. Battery savers may delay them. Exact alarms improve timing.

Usage is synced when you open or resume the app, and about once a minute while it is open. Android's usage history lets Helpful Pets measure the time since your last visit, including while the app was closed.

07

Children

Helpful Pets is not directed to children under 13. Do not use the app if you are under 13, or under the age of digital consent in your country.

08

Changes to this policy

We version this policy and show the version in the app's Privacy & Terms screen. If a change materially affects what is read or stored, the app asks for consent again before you continue. Non-material updates are published here with a new effective date.
